Privacy policy

Applies to the private Google integration named "Home Assistant", owned by Matt Duerr.

Who uses it

Only Matt Duerr's own Google account. The integration is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

Google Calendar events on calendars that account can see. The access is used only to show those events in, and add events from, Matt's own Home Assistant server.

Where the data goes

Calendar data is stored only on that Home Assistant server, on Matt's home network. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page.
